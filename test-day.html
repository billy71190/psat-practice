Test-Day Tips

The week before

  • Follow your school's instructions. Many schools ask students to bring a fully charged laptop or tablet with the Bluebook app installed and to complete exam setup in Bluebook ahead of time. Do this early and ask your counselor if you're unsure.
  • If you want to use your own calculator, make sure it's on the approved list (the Desmos calculator in Bluebook is always available).
  • Shift your sleep schedule so waking up early feels normal.

The night before

  • No cramming. Skim your mistake log, then do something relaxing.
  • Pack: charged device + charger, a couple of pencils or pens (scratch paper is provided), approved calculator (optional), water, and a snack for the break.
  • Get a full night of sleep. It matters more than one more hour of studying.

During the test

  • Module 1 matters most. Doing well there unlocks the harder Module 2, which leads to the highest scores. Work carefully.
  • Answer every question. There's no penalty for guessing. Before time runs out, fill in anything blank.
  • Use Mark for Review (the flag) on anything that takes longer than about 2 minutes, then come back.
  • Keep an eye on the clock, but don't stare at it. You can hide the timer in Bluebook; it will warn you when 5 minutes are left.
  • Reading and Writing: read the question first, predict an answer, and prove it with the text.
  • Math: underline what's being asked, and use Desmos to check your work or solve by graphing.
  • Use the break. Stand up, eat your snack, and drink water. Don't think about the questions you just answered.
  • If a question feels impossible, that's normal. Everyone sees some very hard questions. Guess, flag it, and move on.

After the test

  • Scores usually come out several weeks later through your College Board account. When they arrive, look at the skill breakdown to plan your SAT prep.
