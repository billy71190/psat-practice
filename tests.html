Full-Length Practice Tests

Each test is built like the digital PSAT/NMSQT: Reading and Writing (2 modules × 27 questions, 32 minutes each), then Math (2 modules × 22 questions, 35 minutes each), with an optional break in between. The second module of each section is a bit harder than the first.

Good to know: the real PSAT/NMSQT is adaptive: how you do on Module 1 decides whether Module 2 is easier or harder. These practice tests use a fixed Module 2, so they cover a wide range of difficulty. For the official adaptive experience, take the free full-length tests in College Board's Bluebook app.

All questions on this site are original practice questions written in the style of the test.
